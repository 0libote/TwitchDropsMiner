TWITCH DROPS MINER NEXT

This is an independent community fork of DevilXD/TwitchDropsMiner.
It is not affiliated with Twitch Interactive, Inc.

START
1. Launch Twitch Drops Miner Next.
2. Your browser opens the local dashboard.
3. Use the Twitch authorization link and enter the displayed one-time code.
4. Choose priority or excluded games in Settings, then refresh the inventory.

The app sends only the requests required to progress Twitch Drops; it does not download stream
video or audio. Avoid watching Twitch with the same account while the miner is active because it
can make reported progress unreliable.

Your authorization cookie and settings are private. Do not share the application data directory.
